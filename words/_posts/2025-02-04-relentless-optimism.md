写给那些总能用不同的眼光看待事物的人。
: <span lang="en">Here\'s to those who have always SEEN THINGS DIFFERENTLY.</span>

追随远见而不是近路的人。
: <span lang="en">The ones who FOLLOW A VISION, not a path.</span>

他人追崇第一里的第一，你们思索第一件真正重要的事。
: <span lang="en">Where others perceive FIRST of the firsts as valuable, you value the first thing that actually matters.</span>

他人被所谓的新奇夺走目光的时候，你们聚焦在新方法的意义上。
: <span lang="en">While others are DISTRACTED by the new, you focus on the significance of A WHOLE NEW TAKE.</span>

即使你们看不清楚，你们也从不怀疑我们终将打破常规。
: <span lang="en">Even before you could see how, you never doubted we would CHANGE THINGS.</span>

我们就这样做到了。<br>齐心协力。<br>一次，又一次，又一次，又一次……
: <span lang="en">And then we did.<br>TOGETHER.<br>AGAIN. AND AGIAN. AND AGIAN. AND AGIAN…</span>

让世界前进的是不懈的乐观。
: <span lang="en">RELENTLESS OPTIMISM is what moves the world forward.</span>

因此，继续用不同的眼光看待事物吧。
: <span lang="en">So, keep seeing things DIFFERENTLY.</span>

继续深信总有另一条道路、更好的道路、更广阔的道路。
: <span lang="en">Keep trusting there is always ANOTHER WAY, A BETTER WAY, A BIGGER WAY.</span>

以人为本。<br>消除阻碍。<br>返本还原。
: <span lang="en">One that lifts up humanity.<br>Breaks down our barriers.<br>And HEALS THE LANDSCAPE.</span>

你们就是现有旧世界与未来美好世界之间的「不同」。
: <span lang="en">You are the difference between the WORLD as it was and the BETTER PLACE it will become.</span>

「不同」是我们永远的共同。
: <span lang="en">And DIFFERENT is the one thing about us that will always be the same.</span>
