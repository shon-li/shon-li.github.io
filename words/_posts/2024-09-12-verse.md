读诗写诗不是因为它漂亮。<br>读诗写诗是因为我们是人。<br>人，有热情。
: <span lang="en">We don\'t read and write poetry because it\'s cute.<br>We read and write poetry because we are members of the human race.<br>And the human race is filled with passion.</span>

医学、法学、商学、工程学，<br>都很高尚，<br>靠它们能延续生命。<br>但诗歌、美、浪漫与爱，<br>才是生命的向往。
: <span lang="en">And medicine, law, business, engineering,<br>these are noble pursuits and necessary to sustain life.<br>But poetry, beauty, romance, love,<br>these are what we stay alive for.</span>

惠特曼的诗里写过，<br>「我啊！人生啊！…<br>首尾牵连的迷问啊；<br>永无休止的漠视啊…<br>愚昧漫天的城市啊；<br>这里还有什么美好？<br>我啊，人生啊。」
: <span lang="en">To quote from Whitman,<br>\"O me! O life!...<br>of the questions of these recurring;<br>of the endless trains of the faithless...<br>of cities filled with the foolish;<br>what good amid these,<br>O me, O life?\"</span>

答案：<br>你活着。<br>有生命，有自我。<br>生命的大戏展开着，<br>你也能献诗一句。<br>你的人生就在那，<br>你也能献诗一句。
: <span lang="en">Answer.<br>That you are here –<br>that life exists, and identity;<br>that the powerful play goes on and you may contribute a verse.<br>That the powerful play <i>goes on</i><br>and you may contribute a verse.</span>

你想写一句吗？
: <span lang="en">What will your verse be?</span>
